Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot path does not look like the characteristic Jansen path. The panel numbers give roughly 174 mm of x travel against 181 mm of y travel, so the loop is nearly round, where the real path is about twice as wide as it is tall. The flat segment is a near-vertical edge at the left (x about -117 mm) instead of a flat bottom at the ground. This suggests the wrong assembly configuration, a rotated or mirrored linkage, or wrong link lengths. (frames t=1.67s, t=2.00s, t=2.33s)
- The leg triangle and rods look folded or overlapping, with the foot triangle pointing sideways. The linkage looks collapsed rather than a properly assembled leg, which is consistent with a wrong branch or configuration. (frames t=0.00s, t=3.00s, t=3.33s, t=3.67s)
