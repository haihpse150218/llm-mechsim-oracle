Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Leg triangle W-U-T appears to switch assembly configuration: foot hangs below W in the early frames but the triangle is flipped, with the foot to the right of W, in the later frames. Foot x also jumps about 55 mm between consecutive 30 degree frames (85 mm to 30 mm) and then stays flat. A real Strandbeest foot path is a smooth D shape. (frames t=4.58s, t=5.24s, t=5.89s)
- Foot path is too shallow and not the characteristic flat-bottomed D loop. The foot y varies only about 16 mm (-41 to -57) while x varies about 60 mm, so there is almost no lift or return stroke. This suggests wrong link lengths or a wrong branch. (frames t=0.00s, t=0.65s, t=1.96s, t=3.93s)
