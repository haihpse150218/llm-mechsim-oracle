Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Assembly configuration flips between frames: the leg triangle hangs below the pivots with the foot underneath for crank 330-210 deg, then swings to the right with the foot far right (x ~70-92 mm) for 240-330 deg. The foot x jumps from 85 mm at 330 deg to 29.8 mm at 0 deg (a ~55 mm jump in 30 deg of crank), which is a discontinuous branch change, not smooth continuous motion. (frames t=3.27s, t=5.24s, t=5.89s)
- Foot path is not the characteristic Jansen flat-bottomed D-shaped closed loop: the foot only varies about 16 mm in y (-41 to -57) and the trace is a shallow open banana arc with no proper lift phase. The link proportions or assembly seem wrong. (frames t=0.00s, t=1.96s, t=2.62s, t=4.58s)
