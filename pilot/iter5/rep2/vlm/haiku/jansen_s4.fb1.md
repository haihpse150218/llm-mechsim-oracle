Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot path does not look like the characteristic Jansen D-shaped path: the foot y only varies about 16 mm (-41 to -58) while x spans about 65 mm, and the return stroke is a thin arc almost on top of the stride, with no real foot lift. This suggests wrong link lengths or ratios or a wrong assembly configuration. (frames t=0.00s, t=3.27s, t=4.58s, t=5.89s)
- The frame appears mirrored relative to Jansen's layout (the second fixed pivot X should lie to the left of the crank axle O, but the leg hangs to the right of its pivots and the foot travels in +x while the crank turns CCW). The foot x stays nearly constant at about 28 mm from 90 to 150 deg and then jumps from 92 mm back to 29 mm within 60 deg, so the motion looks wrong. (frames t=0.65s, t=1.31s, t=4.58s, t=5.24s, t=5.89s)
