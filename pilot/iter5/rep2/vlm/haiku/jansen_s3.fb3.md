Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot path is not the characteristic flat-bottomed Jansen curve: it is a nearly round loop (x about -117..57 mm, y about -91..90 mm) whose flat/straight section is on the left side rather than at the bottom, and the loop is roughly as tall as it is wide. This suggests a wrong assembly configuration (mirrored or rotated linkage branch) or wrong link lengths. (frames t=1.67s, t=2.00s, t=2.33s, t=2.67s)
- The leg triangle looks oversized relative to the rest of the linkage, and the foot swings far above the crank axle (y up to 90 mm) at the top of the path. A real Jansen foot stays mostly below the frame and lifts only a short distance. (frames t=1.00s, t=1.33s, t=1.67s)
