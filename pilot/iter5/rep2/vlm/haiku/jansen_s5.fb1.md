Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot path is a roughly round, teardrop-shaped loop (about 108 mm wide by 113 mm tall, with a pointed tail at the bottom) and not the flat-bottomed D-shaped Jansen path, which is clearly wider than it is tall with a long near-straight ground contact. This suggests wrong link lengths or ratios or a wrong assembly configuration. The panel foot_y values (-51 to 62) confirm the large height. (frames t=3.50s, t=4.00s, t=4.50s, t=5.00s, t=5.50s)
- The fixed pivot X sits to the right of the crank axle O at the same height. In Jansen's published layout X is to the left of and slightly below O, so the frame looks mirrored or mis-placed. The legs fold into a bunched, overlapping cluster at some phases. (frames t=0.50s, t=1.50s, t=3.00s)
