Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot path is not the flat-bottomed Jansen walking path. The foot swings in a large loop that goes well above the frame and crank axle (t=1.33 to t=3.00), as if the leg triangle were flipped or in the wrong assembly configuration, or the link lengths were wrong. A real leg keeps the foot below the frame with a flat stance section. (frames t=1.33s, t=1.67s, t=2.00s, t=2.33s, t=2.67s, t=3.00s)
- The upper triangle (purple) and leg triangle (yellow) change orientation completely over one revolution. The purple triangle moves from above the frame to below it, and the leg triangle's apex rotates through nearly 360 degrees. This is inconsistent with the bounded rocking of the Jansen linkage, which suggests a wrong configuration or proportions. (frames t=0.00s, t=2.00s, t=2.67s)
