Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Crank (orange) looks far too long relative to the other links. In Jansen's holy numbers the crank is only about 15 mm against 40-65 mm for the other links, but here it is roughly as long as the rods and over half the leg-triangle side. The crank proportion appears wrong. (frames t=0.00s, t=1.00s, t=3.00s)
- The leg does not trace the Jansen walking path. The yellow leg triangle swings high above and around the frame and rotates through large angles, so the foot loops widely instead of tracing a flat-bottomed stride with a short lift. This suggests a wrong link-length ratio or assembly configuration. The leg also folds above the fixed pivots at t=0.67-1.33. (frames t=0.67s, t=1.00s, t=1.33s)
- The fixed pivots O and X appear almost coincident. Jansen's frame separation is about 38 mm horizontal and 7.8 mm vertical, which should be visibly larger than the crank. (frames t=0.00s, t=1.00s)
