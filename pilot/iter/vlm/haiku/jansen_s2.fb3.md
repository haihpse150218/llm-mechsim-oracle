Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot position jumps discontinuously between consecutive 30-degree steps (about 100 mm between t=1.05s and t=1.57s, and about 90 mm between t=4.71s and t=5.24s) although the crank is small. This indicates the solver flips to the wrong assembly branch, so the leg triangle and upper triangle are mirrored or folded. The mechanism does not move continuously. (frames t=1.05s, t=1.57s, t=4.71s, t=5.24s)
- The linkage collapses into a tangled, folded configuration for crank angles 120 to 270 deg, with the leg triangle overlapping the upper triangle and links. This is not the normal Jansen configuration. The traced foot path is not a clean flat-bottomed loop; it is a crescent with extra loops. (frames t=2.09s, t=2.62s, t=3.14s, t=3.67s, t=4.19s, t=4.71s)
