Visual review report (an engineer looked at 12 rendered frames over one revolution). Problems found:
- Foot path is a tilted, rounded ellipse (x about -56 to -123 mm, y about -62 to -108 mm) with no flat-bottomed ground-contact segment. Over the lower part of the loop (t=1.05 to 2.09) y changes by about 14 mm while x moves about 35 mm along a slanted line. This does not match the characteristic Jansen D-shaped path, so the link proportions or assembly configuration may be wrong. (frames t=1.05s, t=1.40s, t=1.75s, t=2.09s)
- Foot speed is very uneven. The lower stroke is covered in about 4 samples with x moving about 60 mm. The upper return stroke takes about 8 samples at about 10 mm each. The slow part is the lifted return and the fast part is the low stroke, which is the opposite of a walking leg's slow, flat stance. (frames t=1.75s, t=2.09s, t=2.44s, t=2.79s)
