\section{Related work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{LLM-generated 3D and physics code} Benchmarks for interactive scientific demonstrations \cite{chen2025interactscience}, physics animations \cite{wang2026simuscene}, physics-aware 3D scenes \cite{xie2026physcodebench} and 3D worlds \cite{lu2026worldcoder} show that generated programs usually run but often behave wrongly. WorldCoder-Bench also finds that screenshot-based VLM judging correlates poorly with state-level correctness. Generators of articulated objects and CAD models use a VLM critic in the loop \cite{gao2026lam,le2025articulate,alrashedy2025cad}, as does agentic generation of physics simulation code \cite{shende2026perceptual}, which names the remaining ``oracle gap''. SGA verifies educational animations through their scene graph \cite{lopez2026sga}. Closest in domain are LLM-based linkage synthesis \cite{gandarela2025msynth,gandarela2026linkage}, where a trusted simulator evaluates parameters the LLM proposes, and the generation of multibody models compared with expert ground truth \cite{gerstmayr2026meceng}. In contrast, we test the simulator code itself, derive the ground truth from the simulator's declared parameters, and study the oracle as gate and feedback source.

\paragraph{Test oracles and metamorphic testing} Our oracle is a derived oracle in the sense of Barr et al.\ \cite{barr2015oracle}. Its META family consists of metamorphic and structural relations \cite{chen2018metamorphic,segura2016survey} implied by the output contract, analogous to metamorphic testing of graphics compilers \cite{donaldson2017shader}, scientific software \cite{kanewala2014scientific} and autonomous-driving systems \cite{zhou2019driverless}. Work on oracle automation with neural models \cite{dinella2022toga,hossain2023neural,molina2025oracle} generates assertions. We instead encode reusable domain rules once per mechanism type and keep the oracle independent of the model under test, as recommended by Molina et al.\ \cite{molina2025oracle}. Insufficient tests over-accept LLM code \cite{liu2023evalplus}, and property-based tests have been proposed as a remedy \cite{vikram2023pbt}; our setting has no tests at all, so the oracle has to provide them.

\paragraph{Simulation V\&V} Classical simulation V\&V lists both animation and comparison with analytic results among validation techniques \cite{sargent2013vv,balci1997vva,kleijnen1995vv}. Our comparison of VLM review and executable oracle can be read as a comparison of these two techniques, automated, for generated simulators.

\paragraph{Repair with feedback} Self-Debug \cite{chen2023selfdebug}, Reflexion \cite{shinn2023reflexion} and Self-Refine \cite{madaan2023selfrefine} use execution or self-generated feedback. Olausson et al.\ \cite{olausson2024selfrepair} show that feedback quality limits self-repair, and that self-correction without external signal is unreliable \cite{huang2024cannot,kamoi2024selfcorrection}. Among SE approaches, ChatRepair \cite{xia2024chatrepair} repairs in conversation with test-failure feedback until tests pass, and Fan et al.\ \cite{fan2023repairllm} repair LLM-generated programs and find location information useful. ConvCodeWorld \cite{han2025convcodeworld} varies the kind and amount of feedback in conversational code generation, and Pan et al.\ \cite{pan2025gfr} vary model strength in the generator, feedback and refiner roles. To our knowledge, no prior study uses a single deterministic oracle to deliver feedback at controlled levels of information (location with value, location only, check with magnitude), compares them with VLM review and self-refinement under oracle-gated stopping, and reports an interaction between the needed information and the repairing model.

\paragraph{LLM and VLM judges} LLM judges are biased and unreliable for code correctness \cite{zheng2023judging,wang2024notfair,crupi2025llmjudgecode}, and multimodal judges and VLMs struggle with simple geometry and visual glitches \cite{chen2024mllmjudge,rahmanzadehgervi2024blind,taesiri2024glitchbench}. Our VLM results (unstable verdicts, false alarms on intermittent motion, invisible conditions missed) are consistent with this literature in a new domain.

\paragraph{In this journal} Related work in ISSE checks that a rendered block diagram matches its model \cite{waldvogel2026blockdiagram}, classifies AI for software engineering \cite{schieferdecker2026ai4se} and uses LLMs to interpret test logs of a digital twin \cite{hermawan2026log}. We are not aware of an ISSE paper on oracles for LLM-generated simulation code.
